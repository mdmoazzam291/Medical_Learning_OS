# Product scope

## Goal
Help medical learners choose their next useful study action and retain knowledge across exams and clinical training. Initial audience: MBBS students and interns preparing for NEET-PG/INI-CET.

## First usable release
One learner can start a short question session, answer reviewed questions, see explanations, save a concept note, revisit incorrect answers, and resume after reloading. Show observed accuracy and review counts with sample sizes. Keep a manually editable exam date; no official date is assumed.

## Interaction priorities
Primary action: **Study Now**.

Current beta learner navigation is **Home · Study · Exams · Vault · Account**. Revision, QBank, progress and planning are contextual views/actions rather than permanent top-level destinations. A server-authorized **Admin** destination is conditionally visible only to the singleton beta content admin and is not part of the learner information architecture.

Home is a Preparation Command Center: Study Now first, then due/unseen learning signals and direct paths to Study, Exams and NeuralVault. Hide unnecessary controls during study. Support keyboard use, readable contrast, phone and tablet layouts.

## Later systems
Medical Knowledge/Competency Graph; Preparation/Learner Digital Twin; Exam DNA/PYQ; Mistake Fingerprint; Memory Debt; GT Autopsy; multimodal learning; provider-independent AI tutor; adaptive teaching; institutions and educator marketplace.

These names describe planned capabilities, not proven algorithms. Validate their value against simple baselines before expanding.
