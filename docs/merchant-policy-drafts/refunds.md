# Medretica — Refunds & Cancellations

**OWNER REVIEW DRAFT — proposed rules, not approved, effective or published.** Effective date: to be set at approved publication. Paid plans remain unavailable by explicit owner instruction.

## Current unpaid release
Medretica currently offers no paid plan for purchase and does not initiate subscriptions or recurring charges. No payment is required for the currently offered free access. If you see an unexpected Medretica-related charge, contact support@medretica.com with the payment reference; do not pay again or send payment secrets. Investigate any such charge rather than implying no remedy exists because checkout was disabled.

## Proposed rules for a future paid launch
These are business-policy recommendations requiring owner approval and implementation before paid launch; they do not activate paid service.

### Seven-day refund window
Request a full refund within **7 calendar days of the successful initial purchase or renewal payment**. Apply this consistently without a hidden usage threshold. Refund the amount actually collected for that payment, including applicable tax adjustments, through the original payment route where supported. Access attached to that refunded period ends when the refund is confirmed; personal notes/history are not deleted merely because paid access ends. A payment can be refunded only once, and a previous refund or chargeback must be checked to prevent duplicate repayment.

### Duplicate, incorrect or undelivered purchases
Correct duplicate or incorrect charges and resolve non-delivery or a materially defective/not-as-described service through an appropriate refund or other lawful remedy. Do not use the seven-day voluntary window to deny applicable statutory rights. Where the whole purchased service cannot be supplied, propose a full refund; for a partially supplied service, offer a reasonable proportionate remedy with the affected customer. Do not deduct an undisclosed processing/cancellation fee.

### Cancellation and renewal
Customers may cancel future renewal without a cancellation fee. Cancellation recorded before the next renewal falls due stops future renewal billing; paid access continues until the end of the already-paid term unless that term is refunded. Publish the next due date/time and an unambiguous cancellation confirmation before subscriptions launch. Cancellation does not itself refund the current term, but the seven-day rule and other applicable remedies still apply. Outside those grounds, no routine pro-rata refund is proposed for voluntary mid-term cancellation.

Offer an account cancellation control once implemented, with support@medretica.com as the assistance route; never claim that an unbuilt button is available. Cancel the provider subscription/renewal instruction as well as recording the request. Explain mandate revocation separately; do not treat deletion of the learner account as billing cancellation. If a confirmed timely cancellation is followed by an incorrect renewal charge, refund that charge.

### Request and processing
Email **support@medretica.com** with the registered account email, order/payment reference, payment date and request. Administration/escalation: **admin@medretica.com**. Never ask for passwords, OTPs, CVV, API keys or full card details.
- Proposed acknowledgement target: **within 48 hours**.
- Proposed decision target for a complete request: **within 7 business days**, explaining any information needed or exceptional delay.
- Proposed approved-refund initiation target: **within 5 business days after approval**.
- Bank/payment-provider credit time is additional and varies by payment method; supply the refund reference and expected timeframe shown by the actual provider. Do not guarantee an unsupported bank-credit date.
- These targets must be reviewed against staffing and applicable complaint-handling requirements before approval; they do not override a shorter mandatory deadline.

## Future policy changes
Use the [Terms update clause](terms.md#8-changes-to-these-terms): dated versions, notice of material changes, prospective effect and protection for existing purchase rights. A new policy does not retrospectively remove a refund right or itself authorise a debit.

Owner approval of these proposed commitments, public contact/address/grievance details, exact offers, cancellation tooling, trusted payment/refund reconciliation and operational support capacity. This policy does not assert that Cashfree recurring product activation, payment processing or refund execution is ready.
