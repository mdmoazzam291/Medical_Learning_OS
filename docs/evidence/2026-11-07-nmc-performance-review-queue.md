## Review sequence

Start with GM28.11, GM28.17 and GM28.18 to exercise the Respiratory pilot. Use the [existing assessment specifications](./2026-11-07-nmc-foundation-readiness-audit.md) and [exact source mappings](./2026-11-07-nmc-respiratory-pilot-mapping.md). After that, batch by packet and reuse an assessment only when the observable action, context and standard match.

For each candidate, record:

1. The exact NMC reference ID and assessment mode. Check whether the source expects observed demonstration, interpreted findings, explanation, or a combination.
2. The observable action, setting and expected output. Split compound competencies into independently reviewable components and keep unresolved components open.
3. The exact teaching and assessment versions and rubric, with decision criteria and critical omissions. Record whether the learner had assistance and distinguish repeated items from fresh cases.
4. The review disposition: retain the performance tag, revise the draft task, change its mode, or hold for source review. Leave unavailable teaching or assessment material visible as a gap.
5. The separate identity, source-alignment and clinical decisions. A passed knowledge question contributes knowledge evidence. A practical skill requires its corresponding observation.
