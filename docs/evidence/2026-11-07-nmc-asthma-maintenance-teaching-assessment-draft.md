# Asthma maintenance teaching and assessment draft

Original draft for GM28.17, September 2024 NMC PDF page 324. Source reference nmc-2024-ref:a54d769a2db7aa80f396b6137515a35907de9eae3ade9256f63fb3edd876fa4c. Candidate identity: Asthma maintenance treatment. Clinical/source review pending.

## Teaching note

In adults and adolescents, maintenance planning addresses symptoms and future exacerbation risk. GINA recommends ICS-containing treatment. Check diagnosis, inhaler use, adherence, exposures and comorbidities before escalating treatment. Plan education, an action plan and follow-up.

## Original simulated exercises

All cases are invented. Provide the reviewed adult treatment reference alongside the exercise.

## Proposed rubric

Score four criteria independently, 0 for missing/incorrect and 1 for explicit/correct: extracts relevant constraints, checks modifiable contributors, justifies a reference-supported plan, and explains follow-up. Record prompting separately. Review acceptable alternatives and critical errors before setting a pass standard. Preserve separate versions for each case.
