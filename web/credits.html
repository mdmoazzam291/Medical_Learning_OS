Medretica QBank · Series 1

Medretica QBank · Series 1 contains questions adapted from the MedMCQA dataset by Ankit Pal, Logesh Kumar Umapathi and Malaikannan Sankarasubbu (2022), published under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Medretica has reformatted these questions, organised them by subject and NMC competency, and added its own labels. Answer keys in Series 1 have not yet been independently verified. Series 1 is being replaced step by step with questions written by Medretica.
