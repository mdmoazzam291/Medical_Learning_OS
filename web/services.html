Your Medical Learning Operating System.

Medretica is an integrated medical learning platform for medical education and exam preparation. It brings study, practice, personal notes, revision and progress into one connected learning workspace.

A connected approach to preparation

  • Study and practice: use the available study and exam workflows with the reviewed content currently available.
  • Notes and revision: organise personal learning and revisit concepts, mistakes and due reviews.
  • Progress: view recorded practice activity to help choose a next useful study action. Practice accuracy is not a validated mastery score or a predicted rank.

Content availability depends on what has actually been reviewed and published. An empty collection does not imply a paid question bank is available.

Current access and pricing

Current free access: INR 0.

Paid plans, checkout and recurring subscriptions are unavailable. There is no payment or AutoPay mandate to activate.

Pro, AI Pro and advanced AI or adaptive features shown as proposals or previews are not active purchase offers. Future prices, limits and inclusions will be published before any paid launch.

Where to use Medretica

Currently available through the website. A mobile app is planned; no app-store availability or launch date is promised here.

“Medical Learning Operating System” describes our connected learning platform. It is not a device operating system, clinical diagnosis service or treatment service. No exam result, rank or admission is guaranteed.
