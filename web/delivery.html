MEDRETICA · SERVICE INFORMATION

Digital Service Delivery

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Version 1.0
Applies from publication on this website.

Medretica is a digital learning service accessed through the website.

Access to the current service

Use the website and, where required, sign in to your learner account. Internet access and a supported browser are needed. No physical products are shipped under this service.

Study tools and content are available only to the extent actually released, reviewed and published. Preview features and future roadmap items are not promised deliveries. Where content is unavailable, the site may show an empty collection.

No paid fulfilment at present

Paid plans and recurring subscriptions remain unavailable. Before selling access, we will disclose the exact inclusions, INR price, term, limits, access-start timeline and applicable refund and cancellation rules.

A browser return screen or a pending bank message alone must not be treated as successful payment or verified paid access.

Access difficulties

If the website or your account does not work as expected, email support@medretica.com with the relevant time and error description. Do not include patient information or authentication/payment secrets.

See Refunds & Cancellations for unexpected-charge concerns.
