Information used for the service

  • Account information: email, sign-in/session information and account settings needed for authentication and access.
  • Learning information: the answers, practice activity, revision records, progress and preferences associated with the features you use.
  • Your submissions: notes, files, annotations or feedback you choose to submit through available tools.
  • Support and technical information: correspondence and diagnostic/security information needed to investigate issues and operate the website.

Do not submit patient-identifying information or payment/authentication secrets. Collecting patient records is not a purpose of this learning platform.

How information is used

Information supports sign-in, account administration, saving your learning activity, study/revision features, security and resolving requests. Optional features should explain any additional processing before you choose to use them. This notice does not enrol you in research or authorise unrelated AI training or marketing.

Browser storage and service providers

Browser storage can retain account sessions and device preferences. Clearing it may sign you out or remove device-only settings; it does not erase server-held records.

The service uses external infrastructure for hosting, authentication, storage and technical operation, including Cloudflare, Supabase and Render. Where preview error monitoring is enabled, Sentry processes diagnostic information with configured redaction. Payment collection is currently unavailable. Access by service providers is subject to the role they perform; we may also disclose information where required by applicable law or to address a genuine security issue.

Security measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Do not include secrets or patient information in support messages or diagnostic screenshots.

Retention and privacy requests

Account and learning records are retained to provide your continuing workspace and learning history. Support and technical records may be retained as needed to resolve issues, protect the service and meet applicable obligations. Any permitted retention after a deletion request must be limited to an applicable operational or legal need; deletion of active records and expiry of backup copies are different processes.

Contact support to request access, correction or deletion, to raise a privacy concern or to withdraw an applicable optional consent. We may need proportionate verification that the request concerns your account. We will explain applicable limits or further steps rather than promise immediate deletion from every system.

Clearing browser storage, cancelling any future subscription and closing an account are separate actions. If a child has provided information without appropriate authorisation, a parent or guardian should contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Updates to this notice

We may update this notice as service, legal or processing arrangements change. We will date the revised version and give clear notice of material changes. A general policy update does not itself authorise unrelated new uses of personal data; any further required consent must be obtained. Mandatory privacy rights remain unaffected.
